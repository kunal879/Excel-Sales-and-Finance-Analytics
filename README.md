# Excel-Sales-and-Finance-Analytics

Atliq Hardwares - FY 2021 Market Performance Analysis
This project presents a financial performance overview for Atliq Hardwares across various global markets for the fiscal year 2021. The primary focus is on analyzing Net Sales, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Gross Margin, and Gross Margin Percentage (GM%) across 23 different markets.

Key Highlights:
Top Markets by Net Sales:

India leads significantly with $161.26M, followed by the USA ($87.78M) and South Korea ($48.97M).

Highest Gross Margin %:

New Zealand (48.2%), Japan (46.5%), and the United Kingdom (45.1%) recorded the highest profitability percentages.

Lowest Gross Margin %:

Germany (26.2%) and Norway (29.5%) had the least efficient cost-to-profit ratios.

Market Insights:

Some smaller markets like Sweden and Austria posted low sales figures but maintained relatively healthy margin percentages.

Mature markets such as France, UK, and USA combined solid sales volumes with strong gross margins, making them key revenue generators.


Scenario Planning Tool for Customer Croma
This project is focused on financial scenario analysis for a product sold to Customer Croma, with projections dated 12-05-2025. The tool evaluates potential outcomes under three distinct business scenarios: Best Case, Realistic, and Worst Case. It assesses financial impact based on units sold, discount rates, cost of goods sold (COGS), and pricing parameters.

Key Financial Assumptions:
Discount: 10%

COGS: 30% of Net Invoice Price

Average Net Invoice Price (NIP): $6.00 per unit

Gross Margin (GM) Target: $100,000

Purpose:
This tool helps in strategic decision-making by simulating possible financial outcomes under varying sales volumes. It assists in evaluating risk, setting performance benchmarks, and planning resource allocation effectively to meet or exceed gross margin targets.

AtliQ Hardwares – Sales Market Report
This project analyzes the financial performance of AtliQ Hardwares across various international markets. The key focus is on understanding sales dynamics using metrics like Net Sales, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), Gross Margin, and Gross Margin Percentage (GM%).

Key Financial Overview:
Total Net Sales: $883.0M

Total COGS: $555.3M

Total Gross Margin: $327.7M

Overall Gross Margin %: 0.4%

Observations:
Despite a high cumulative gross margin, most individual markets show negative gross margin percentages, indicating high COGS relative to sales in those areas.

India leads in revenue with $241.9M in net sales but has a marginal gross margin of -1.3%.

USA is the second-highest revenue generator at $131.2M, with a slightly better margin of -3.2%.

Markets like Sweden (-270.3%), Austria (-186.7%), and Poland (-65.2%) exhibit extremely poor profitability.

Only the overall total shows a positive margin due to aggregation, suggesting either errors in per-market allocations or differences in how COGS is treated globally.

Purpose:
The report supports market performance assessment, helping stakeholders identify where the company is overspending or underpricing. It is crucial for profitability diagnostics, strategic budgeting, and regional cost management planning.
